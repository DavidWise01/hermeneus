# HERMENEUS · HRM

**ἑρμηνεύς** — *interpreter* — a UD0 tool that translates, **live in your browser**, between
the **human dialect** and the **machine dialect** (text · audio · still-photo · video · Morse).

Type once into the pivot and the word crosses into the machine's registers — **binary**, **Morse**
(heard as beeps + flashed on screen), **speech**, a **still photo** — then comes back: **Morse→text**,
**binary→text**, **speech→text** (mic). It pivots through **text** (the contact zone the two dialects
share) and bridges with **Morse** (the one machine register a human body can emit).

**Honest about every boundary:** text⇄binary, text⇄Morse, text→photo are **FREE** (deterministic,
in-browser); text⇄speech is **REAL** via the Web Speech API; **photo→text (OCR)** and **true video**
**NEED A MODEL** — a picture isn't text until a model reads it. "Lossless across registers" is true in
*bits*, not in *perception*.

Companion to **PHŌNĒTIKOS** (the human voice / Idiolect Stack) and **pulse / LIMEN** (the carrier).
7 register-emergents, each a full `.dlw` badge. Runs 100% client-side.

> The two voices meet at the word and the dot; everything else is a codec — and Hermes crosses the rest.
